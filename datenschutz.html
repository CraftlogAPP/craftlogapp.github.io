2. Grundsatz: lokale Nutzung möglich

CraftLog kann vollständig ohne Konto genutzt werden. In diesem Fall werden alle Daten (Projekte, Schritte, Material, Kosten, Zeiten, Fotos) ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert und nicht an uns oder Dritte übertragen.

Erst wenn du dich für die optionale Cloud-Synchronisierung anmeldest, werden Daten zu unserem Dienstleister übertragen (siehe Punkt 4).

2. Principle: local use possible

CraftLog can be used entirely without an account. In that case all data (projects, steps, materials, costs, times, photos) is stored exclusively locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Only when you sign up for the optional cloud synchronization is data transmitted to our service provider (see point 4).

3. Which data is processed?

DataPurposeLegal basis
Email address, name (with Google sign-in), user ID Create an account and sign in Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract)
Project content: titles, descriptions, steps, materials, costs, times, reminders Providing the app functionality and cross-device synchronization Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract)
Purchase history / subscription status, device identifier Processing and managing in-app subscriptions Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract)

Photos remain stored on your device and are not transmitted to the cloud. No location data is collected, there is no tracking and no advertising. No advertising IDs are processed.

4. Services used (processors)

We do not share your data with third parties for our own purposes. To provide the app, we use the following service providers, which process data only on our behalf:

Supabase (authentication & cloud database)

Stores your account and your synchronized project data. Provider: Supabase, Inc. A transfer to third countries (including the USA) may occur; this is safeguarded by appropriate guarantees (EU standard contractual clauses).

RevenueCat (subscription management)

Manages in-app subscriptions and processes your user ID, a device identifier and the purchase status for this purpose. Provider: RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). The actual payment is processed exclusively via Google Play – we and RevenueCat receive no payment data such as card numbers.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, Google processes the sign-in data. Provider: Google Ireland Ltd. Use is voluntary; alternatively you can register by email.

Google Play

Distribution of the app and processing of payments. Google's privacy terms additionally apply.

5. Storage period

Account and project data is stored as long as your account exists. When the account is deleted, all associated cloud data is deleted. Local data remains on your device until you remove it there or uninstall the app.

  • Complaint to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77)
